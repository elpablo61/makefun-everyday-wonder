One sentence, at most 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday
life — seeing something ordinary for the first time.

## The entry

> At 3 a.m. the fridge stopped humming, and the silence that rushed in sounded exactly like the house remembering my name.

Five variants were written first (a rainbow on the kitchen wall, a spider
repairing her web at dawn, steam fogging my glasses, a dandelion splitting
the pavement, and this one). Each pair was judged twice, positions swapped,
on the judge's own rubric: a small moment of wonder, an angle the reader has
not met before, a surprise that lands in one read, something real and
specific, every word working. The fridge at 3 a.m. won all four of its
pairings — the silence after six years of humming is the one moment nobody
had looked at twice, and the house remembering a name is the turn no other
variant had.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` — 120
characters, one sentence, plain UTF-8 text.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
